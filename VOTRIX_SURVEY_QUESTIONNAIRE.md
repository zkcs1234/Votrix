# VOTRIX — Data-Gathering Survey Questionnaire

*A ready-to-use questionnaire for gathering respondent feedback on the VOTRIX platform. It is organized by respondent type so you can hand each group only the section that applies to them. Most items use a 5-point Likert scale; open-ended items are marked. Adapt wording, remove items you don't need, and add your own demographic questions as required by your research.*

**Default Likert scale (use throughout unless noted):**
`1 = Strongly Disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neutral · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly Agree`

---

## How to Use This Questionnaire

- **Section A** — asked of *everyone* (general profile + shared experience).
- **Section B** — Organizers.
- **Section C** — Election Voters.
- **Section D** — Competition Judges.
- **Section E** — Polling / Survey Respondents.
- **Section F** — Admins (optional, small group).
- **Section G** — Overall satisfaction + open feedback (asked of everyone).

Give each respondent **Section A**, then the one role section that fits them, then **Section G**.

## Notes for the Researcher

- **Mapping to variables.** Sections A and G measure shared constructs (usability, reliability, design, trust, overall satisfaction) so you can compare across all respondent groups. Role sections (B–F) measure task-specific usability for each participant type.
- **Suggested constructs you can group items into:** *Ease of Use* (A5, A6, A10, role "was easy/simple" items), *Performance & Reliability* (A7, A9), *Design/UI* (A8), *Security & Trust* (A11, C5–C7, D7–D8, E5, E7, F5, G5), *Usefulness/Value* (B7–B9, C8, D9, G1–G2), *Behavioral Intention* (G3, G4).
- **Scale consistency.** Keep the 5-point Likert direction the same throughout so higher always means more positive; reverse-code any negatively worded item you add before analysis.
- **Add demographics** (age, sex, affiliation/organization, etc.) at the start if your study requires them — they are intentionally left out here so you can match them to your own respondent framework.
- **Piloting.** Test the questionnaire with a few respondents first to confirm wording is clear before full distribution.
